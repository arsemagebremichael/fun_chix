DNA survival

The game has 3 players at the start, each with a different species All players are aiming to achieve the top species. At the beginning of the game, each player is assigned gene coins, which they use to survive in environments or buy environments as territories. The board has 40 boxes around the square board, with each corner having a Start, a stasis, a free spot, and a go-to stasis. Two dice are rolled, and the total of each is the number of steps players take. Each box has a different property, with different features, a good chance, a bad chance, and an environment. The environments are buyable with gene coins, and once you own an environment, the effects of such an environment do not affect you. Any other player passing through the territory is affected, and based on the environment, they lose points. The players can, however, sell points to the environment owner so that they survive. Landing on a chance (Good or Bad) earns the player a card of benefit or doom. A tax system is imposed on every player based on their genetic values, i.e, stronger genes lead to higher income tax, and a general tax gives RTA(random tax allocation) If you roll the dice and it gives you the same number thrice, you go to stasis. To save yourself from stasis, you pay a bail of a stated amount. Gaining more gene coins and different genes while surviving various environments helps the players evolve to become stronger creatures. Different levels of points give different levels of genetic advantage. E.g., when a player earns 600 gene coins, they automatically gain speed. One will only win the game if everyone else loses their gene coins and dies out due to the inability to survive
